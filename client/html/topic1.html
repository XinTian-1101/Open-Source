🌡️ Understanding Climate Change

This topic introduces the key concepts around climate change as below :

1.0 What is Climate Change ?

Climate change refers to the long-term alteration of temperature and typical weather patterns in a place. Over the past century, human activities have dramatically altered Earth's climate. While natural factors, such as volcanic eruptions and solar variations, have played a role in climate shifts throughout Earth's history, the current period of rapid warming is primarily due to human activities, particularly the burning of fossil fuels and deforestation. Climate change is not just about warming temperatures; it encompasses a range of impacts, including changes in precipitation patterns, sea level rise, and shifting ecosystems. These changes have significant consequences for human societies, wildlife, and natural ecosystems.

2.0 The Role of Greenhouse Gases

Greenhouse gases (GHGs) are atmospheric gases that trap heat from the Sun, warming Earth's surface. This phenomenon is known as the "greenhouse effect." While this process is natural and necessary to sustain life on Earth, human activities have greatly amplified the concentration of these gases, leading to an enhanced greenhouse effect and global warming.

The primary greenhouse gases include carbon dioxide (CO₂), methane (CH₄), nitrous oxide (N₂O), and fluorinated gases. Among these, carbon dioxide is the most significant in terms of its impact on global warming.

  • Carbon Dioxide (CO₂): The largest contributor to global warming, CO₂ is primarily released through the burning of fossil fuels like coal, oil, and natural gas, as well as through deforestation.
  • Methane (CH₄): While methane is less abundant than CO₂, it is much more potent as a greenhouse gas. It is released during the production and transport of coal, oil, and natural gas, as well as from livestock and decaying organic waste.
  • Nitrous Oxide (N₂O): Released from agricultural practices, industrial activities, and the combustion of fossil fuels. Though less abundant, it is a powerful greenhouse gas.

The increasing concentration of these gases in the atmosphere traps more heat and disrupts Earth's natural climate systems, leading to global temperature rises, extreme weather events, and other severe consequences.

3.0 Impacts of Climate Change

The impacts of climate change are wide-ranging. Some of the most significant include:

  • Rising Temperatures: Global temperatures have increased, leading to heatwaves and changing weather patterns.
  • Sea Level Rise: Melting glaciers and thermal expansion of seawater are causing sea levels to rise, threatening coastal areas.
  • Extreme Weather: More frequent hurricanes, floods, and droughts are becoming a reality in many regions.

These changes affect ecosystems, agriculture, and human health, creating challenges for societies worldwide.
